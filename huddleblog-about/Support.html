Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

You can create an account by opening the Huddle app and tapping the login button. No password is required - just tap to get started!

How do I post content?

To post content, tap the post button in the app. You can take photos or select images from your gallery (up to 6 images), add filters, and write a description with tags.

How do I edit my profile?

Go to your profile page and tap the settings icon. From there, you can update your profile picture, username, and bio.

How do I report inappropriate content?

If you encounter inappropriate content, you can report it by tapping on the post and selecting the report option. Our team will review it promptly.

How do I delete my account?

To delete your account, go to Settings and select "Delete Account". Please note that this action cannot be undone.

How do I change my privacy settings?

You can manage your privacy settings in the Settings section of the app. There you can control who can see your posts and contact you.
